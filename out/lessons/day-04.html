DAY 04 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Tools and collaboration

Giải thích công cụ và cách bạn phối hợp.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Describe two tools you use and who you collaborate with. Speak for 60–90 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 3 · ôn sau 1 bài

Tell a colleague about your current project. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 1 · ôn sau 3 bài

Explain the main thing you are responsible for. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 3 · ôn sau 1 bài

Say what you are doing at this moment. Say one sentence from memory.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

I'm Sam. I mainly work with the front-end team, and I'm currently working on a new page. We use Slack to ask quick questions. I collaborate with a designer to make the page clear. We keep track of our tasks on a shared board. I share designs with the product manager before we start building. When something is unclear, I ask for more information. The deadline is Friday, so this saves time. At the end of the day, I check the board and send an update. It's going well because everyone knows what to do next.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is Sam explaining?
2. Where does the team track tasks?
3. Why does Sam ask for more information?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We use… to…

Chúng tôi dùng… để…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain the purpose of a tool you use.
Pattern
We use + tool + to + base verb
Simple example
We use chat to ask questions.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We use a shared calendar to plan meetings.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I collaborate with…

Tôi cộng tác với…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say who you work together with on a task.
Pattern
I collaborate with + person / team
Simple example
I collaborate with the design team.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I collaborate with a designer on the payment page.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We keep track of…

Chúng tôi theo dõi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain what information your team tracks.
Pattern
We keep track of + noun
Simple example
We keep track of our tasks.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We keep track of customer problems on a shared board.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I share… with…

Tôi chia sẻ… với…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain who receives your work or information.
Pattern
I share + thing + with + person
Simple example
I share notes with my team.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I share test results with the developers.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

When something is unclear…

Khi có điều gì chưa rõ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain what you do when you need more information.
Pattern
When something is unclear, I + verb
Simple example
When something is unclear, I ask.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
When something is unclear, I ask our product manager.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. We use… to…

2. I collaborate with…

3. We keep track of…

4. I share… with…

5. When something is unclear…

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

We use chat to ask questions. /
I collaborate with the design team. /
We keep track of our tasks.

Speaking challenge: Describe two tools you use and who you collaborate with.

Speak for 60–90 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What do you do in your team?

Colleague · question 2

What are you working on now?

Colleague · question 3

What is difficult about it?

Colleague · question 4

What will you do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Explain the purpose of a tool you use.
2. Explain what you do when you need more information.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
