DAY 13 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Comparing solutions

Đề xuất và so sánh hai giải pháp.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Compare two solutions and recommend one. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 12 · ôn sau 1 bài

Invite someone to check something with you. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 10 · ôn sau 3 bài

Offer one explanation to investigate. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 6 · ôn sau 7 bài

Start a friendly conversation about progress. Say two connected sentences aloud.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We're having an issue with the old service. I've already tried restarting it, but it didn't solve the issue. One option is to repair it today. The advantage is that we can finish quickly. However, the downside is that the same problem may return. Another option is to replace the service. That takes more time, but it will be easier to maintain. I'd recommend fixing it today and planning a replacement next week. The impact is a long wait for customers, so we need a short-term solution while we prepare something better.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker comparing?
2. Which option can be finished quickly?
3. Why recommend a repair first?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

One option is…

Một lựa chọn là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Present a possible solution.
Pattern
One option is to + base verb
Simple example
One option is to fix it.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
One option is to repair the old service.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Another option is…

Một lựa chọn khác là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Present an alternative solution.
Pattern
Another option is to + base verb
Simple example
Another option is to replace it.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Another option is to build a simpler service.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The advantage is…

Ưu điểm là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain the benefit of an option.
Pattern
The advantage is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
The advantage is the lower cost.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The advantage is that we can finish today.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

However, the downside is…

Tuy nhiên, nhược điểm là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain a drawback after a benefit.
Pattern
However, the downside is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
However, the downside is the cost.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
However, the downside is that the problem may return.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'd recommend…

Tôi đề xuất…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Recommend an action after considering the options.
Pattern
I'd recommend + noun / verb-ing
Simple example
I'd recommend a short test.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'd recommend fixing the service today.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. One option is…

2. Another option is…

3. The advantage is…

4. However, the downside is…

5. I'd recommend…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Present a possible solution. Say one sentence aloud.

Present an alternative solution. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain the benefit of an option. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain a drawback after a benefit. Say one sentence aloud.

Recommend an action after considering the options. Say one sentence aloud.

Ask for a more precise explanation. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

One option is to fix it. /
Another option is to replace it. /
The advantage is the lower cost.

Speaking challenge: Compare two solutions and recommend one.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What problem are you seeing?

Colleague · question 2

Who is affected?

Colleague · question 3

What have you checked so far?

Colleague · question 4

What do you suggest we do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Present a possible solution.
2. Recommend an action after considering the options.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
Chưa hoàn thành
REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
