DAY 03 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Current tasks

Nói về việc đang làm ngay lúc này.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Explain what you are working on now and what comes next. Speak for 60–90 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

I'm Nora, and I'm part of the product team. I'm currently working on a new payment page. Right now, I'm fixing two small bugs. This week, I'm focusing on making the page work well on phones. I usually start by checking the latest test results. After that, I test one change at a time. It's going well because a colleague is helping me. The deadline is Friday. At the end of the day, I share what I have finished. I help our team understand what still needs work, so there are no surprises.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is Nora's current project?
2. When is the deadline?
3. Why does Nora share her progress?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm currently working on…

Tôi hiện đang làm…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tell a colleague about your current project.
Pattern
I'm currently working on + noun
Simple example
I'm currently working on a new page.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm currently working on our payment page.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Right now, I'm…

Ngay lúc này tôi đang…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say what you are doing at this moment.
Pattern
Right now, I'm + verb-ing
Simple example
Right now, I'm testing the app.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Right now, I'm fixing a small login problem.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

This week, I'm focusing on…

Tuần này tôi tập trung vào…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name your main priority for this week.
Pattern
This week, I'm focusing on + noun / verb-ing
Simple example
This week, I'm focusing on testing.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
This week, I'm focusing on making the app easier to use.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It's going well

Mọi việc đang thuận lợi

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tell someone that your task is progressing well.
Pattern
It's going well + optional reason with because
Simple example
It's going well.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It's going well because the team is helping me.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The deadline is…

Hạn chót là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tell someone when a task must be ready.
Pattern
The deadline is + time / date
Simple example
The deadline is Friday.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The deadline is Friday afternoon, so I'm testing today.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I'm currently working on…

2. Right now, I'm…

3. This week, I'm focusing on…

4. It's going well

5. The deadline is…

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I'm currently working on a new page. /
Right now, I'm testing the app. /
This week, I'm focusing on testing.

Speaking challenge: Explain what you are working on now and what comes next.

Speak for 60–90 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What do you do in your team?

Colleague · question 2

What are you working on now?

Colleague · question 3

What is difficult about it?

Colleague · question 4

What will you do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
