DAY 20 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Follow-up questions

Trả lời và xử lý câu hỏi tiếp theo.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Answer two follow-up questions about one of your ideas. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 19 · ôn sau 1 bài

Explain why your team selected an option. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 17 · ôn sau 3 bài

Disagree gently without attacking the speaker. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 13 · ôn sau 7 bài

Explain the benefit of an option. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 6 · ôn sau 14 bài

Suggest checking a problem together. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Manager: How long will the test take, and what will it cost?
Tom: That's a good question. The test should take two days. What I mean is that one small team will try the new process first.
Manager: Why use a small team?
Tom: To give you an example, our last small test helped us find a problem before everyone used the tool. I think that reduces risk.
Manager: And the cost?
Tom: I don't have that number yet. I'll check and get back to you this afternoon. We considered a bigger test, but the main reason for starting small is to learn quickly.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is Tom doing?
2. Which information does Tom still need?
3. Why does Tom promise to reply later?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

That's a good question

Đó là câu hỏi hay

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Acknowledge a follow-up question before answering.
Pattern
That's a good question + response
Simple example
That's a good question. Let me check.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
That's a good question. The test will take two days.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

What I mean is…

Ý tôi là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Clarify something you said earlier.
Pattern
What I mean is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
What I mean is that we need time.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
What I mean is that we should test with one small team.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

To give you an example…

Để lấy một ví dụ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Make your explanation concrete.
Pattern
To give you an example, + clause
Simple example
To give you an example, this page is slow.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
To give you an example, our last test took two days.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I don't have that number yet

Tôi chưa có con số đó

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say honestly that a requested figure is not available.
Pattern
I don't have that number yet + next action
Simple example
I don't have that number yet.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I don't have that number yet. I'll ask the project manager.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'll check and get back to you

Tôi sẽ kiểm tra rồi báo lại

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Promise to verify information and reply.
Pattern
I'll check and get back to you + time
Simple example
I'll check and get back to you.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'll check and get back to you before our next meeting.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. That's a good question

2. What I mean is…

3. To give you an example…

4. I don't have that number yet

5. I'll check and get back to you

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Acknowledge a follow-up question before answering. Say one sentence aloud.

Clarify something you said earlier. Say one sentence aloud.

Make your explanation concrete. Say one sentence aloud.

Say honestly that a requested figure is not available. Say one sentence aloud.

Promise to verify information and reply. Say one sentence aloud.

Give the reason for a past decision. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

That's a good question. Let me check. /
What I mean is that we need time. /
To give you an example, this page is slow.

Speaking challenge: Answer two follow-up questions about one of your ideas.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What would you recommend?

Colleague · question 2

Why do you prefer that option?

Colleague · question 3

Can you give me an example?

Colleague · question 4

What concern should we consider?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
