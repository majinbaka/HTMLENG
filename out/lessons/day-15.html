DAY 15 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Giving an opinion

Nêu quan điểm đơn giản và lịch sự.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Give your opinion about remote or office work. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

BEFORE PRACTICE · 1 MIN

Làm bài nói đầu tuần trước khi đọc bài và xem ví dụ. Ghi lại những gì bạn tự nói được.

Weekly baseline

Repeat this same 60-second task at the beginning and end of the week: Recommend one change at work and give a reason and an example.

Làm riêng với bài nói chính nếu thời lượng khác. Tự đếm số lần dừng lâu hơn 3 giây; không suy ra độ chính xác từ thời lượng.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 14 · ôn sau 1 bài

Identify who or what is affected. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 12 · ôn sau 3 bài

Check the condition in which a problem occurs. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 8 · ôn sau 7 bài

Say when a problem was first observed. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 1 · ôn sau 14 bài

Explain how you contribute to the team. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We're discussing how often to work in the office. I think two office days each week are useful. In my opinion, we can solve difficult questions faster when we're together. From my point of view, we also need quiet time. I prefer working at home because I can focus on testing. For me, a clear plan matters more than the place. We use chat to ask quick questions, and I share updates with my team. One option is to try two office days for a month. After that, we can ask everyone what worked well.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker giving an opinion about?
2. Why does the speaker like working at home?
3. Why suggest a one-month trial?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I think…

Tôi nghĩ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Give a simple opinion in a team discussion.
Pattern
I think + clause
Simple example
I think this is useful.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I think two office days a week are enough.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

In my opinion…

Theo ý kiến của tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Make it clear you are sharing an opinion.
Pattern
In my opinion, + clause
Simple example
In my opinion, this works well.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
In my opinion, remote work helps me focus.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

From my point of view…

Từ góc nhìn của tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain how you see the situation.
Pattern
From my point of view, + clause
Simple example
From my point of view, it's fair.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
From my point of view, the team needs more quiet time.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I prefer… because…

Tôi thích… hơn vì…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
State a preference and give a reason.
Pattern
I prefer + noun / verb-ing + because + clause
Simple example
I prefer chat because it's quick.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I prefer working at home because I can focus.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

For me…

Với tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain what matters to you personally.
Pattern
For me, + clause
Simple example
For me, the price matters.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
For me, a quiet place is important for difficult tasks.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I think…

2. In my opinion…

3. From my point of view…

4. I prefer… because…

5. For me…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Give a simple opinion in a team discussion. Say one sentence aloud.

Make it clear you are sharing an opinion. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain how you see the situation. Say one sentence aloud.

State a preference and give a reason. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain what matters to you personally. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe the first action in your investigation. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I think this is useful. /
In my opinion, this works well. /
From my point of view, it's fair.

Speaking challenge: Give your opinion about remote or office work.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What would you recommend?

Colleague · question 2

Why do you prefer that option?

Colleague · question 3

Can you give me an example?

Colleague · question 4

What concern should we consider?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Which chunk means “Tôi nghĩ…”?
2. Which chunk means “Với tôi…”?
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
Chưa hoàn thành
REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
