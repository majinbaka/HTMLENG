DAY 22 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

What happened last week

Kể về công việc đã xảy ra trong quá khứ.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Describe three things that happened at work last week. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

BEFORE PRACTICE · 1 MIN

Làm bài nói đầu tuần trước khi đọc bài và xem ví dụ. Ghi lại những gì bạn tự nói được.

Weekly baseline

Repeat this same 60-second task at the beginning and end of the week: Describe recent work, a lesson learned, and your next plan.

Làm riêng với bài nói chính nếu thời lượng khác. Tự đếm số lần dừng lâu hơn 3 giây; không suy ra độ chính xác từ thời lượng.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 21 · ôn sau 1 bài

Support your point with a concrete example. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 19 · ôn sau 3 bài

Report a decision your team made. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 15 · ôn sau 7 bài

Explain what matters to you personally. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 8 · ôn sau 14 bài

Describe the condition that triggers a problem. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Last week, I talked to several customers about our app. I completed a short survey and shared the results with my team. We found that users wanted a simpler menu. On Friday, we met to discuss a new design. The result was a clear plan for the first change. I think the survey helped because we heard the same problem from different people. For example, some users could not find the help page. We decided to move it closer to the main screen. This week, I'm focusing on testing that change with two customers before we release it.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker summarizing?
2. What did users want?
3. Why test the change with customers?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Last week, I…

Tuần trước tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Start a short account of last week's work.
Pattern
Last week, I + past-tense verb
Simple example
Last week, I tested the app.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Last week, I talked to five customers.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I completed…

Tôi đã hoàn thành…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Report a finished task.
Pattern
I completed + noun
Simple example
I completed the report.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I completed the first version of the new menu.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We found that…

Chúng tôi nhận thấy rằng…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Report something your team discovered.
Pattern
We found that + clause
Simple example
We found that the page was slow.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We found that users wanted a simpler menu.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

On Friday…

Vào thứ Sáu…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say what happened on a specific day.
Pattern
On Friday, + past-tense clause
Simple example
On Friday, we met the client.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
On Friday, our team agreed to change the design.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The result was…

Kết quả là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe the outcome of past work.
Pattern
The result was + noun / that + clause
Simple example
The result was a shorter wait.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The result was that users could find the menu faster.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. Last week, I…

2. I completed…

3. We found that…

4. On Friday…

5. The result was…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Start a short account of last week's work. Say one sentence aloud.

Report a finished task. Say one sentence aloud.

Report something your team discovered. Say one sentence aloud.

Say what happened on a specific day. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe the outcome of past work. Say one sentence aloud.

Say which part of an opinion you agree with. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

Last week, I tested the app. /
I completed the report. /
We found that the page was slow.

Speaking challenge: Describe three things that happened at work last week.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What did you finish recently?

Colleague · question 2

What happened during the work?

Colleague · question 3

What did you learn?

Colleague · question 4

What are you planning next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Which chunk means “Tuần trước tôi…”?
2. Which chunk means “Kết quả là…”?
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
