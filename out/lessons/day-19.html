DAY 19 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Explaining a decision

Giải thích quyết định và tiêu chí.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Explain a work decision and the reason behind it. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 18 · ôn sau 1 bài

Compare one option with another. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 16 · ôn sau 3 bài

Support your point with a concrete example. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 12 · ôn sau 7 bài

Ask someone to demonstrate or show something. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 5 · ôn sau 14 bài

Name something that is stopping your progress. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We decided to launch a smaller version first. We chose this because we wanted customer feedback early. The reason was that we needed to learn before spending more time on extra features. We considered waiting for the full version, but that would take another month. Although the first version has fewer features, we can still solve the main customer problem. I think this is the better choice because it reduces risk. For example, we can test the new menu with real users this week. After that, we can decide which change will help them most.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What decision did the team make?
2. Why did they choose this?
3. Why mention the new menu?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We decided to…

Chúng tôi đã quyết định…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Report a decision your team made.
Pattern
We decided to + base verb
Simple example
We decided to wait.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We decided to launch a smaller version.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We chose this because…

Chúng tôi chọn cách này vì…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain why your team selected an option.
Pattern
We chose this because + clause
Simple example
We chose this because it's simpler.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We chose this because we need customer feedback soon.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The reason was…

Lý do là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Give the reason for a past decision.
Pattern
The reason was + noun / that + clause
Simple example
The reason was the cost.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The reason was that the team needed more time.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We considered…

Chúng tôi đã cân nhắc…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Mention another option you discussed.
Pattern
We considered + noun / verb-ing
Simple example
We considered waiting.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We considered adding more features before launch.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Although…, we…

Mặc dù…, chúng tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain a decision that has a drawback.
Pattern
Although + clause, we + clause
Simple example
Although it costs more, we chose it.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Although the version is small, we can learn from users.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. We decided to…

2. We chose this because…

3. The reason was…

4. We considered…

5. Although…, we…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Report a decision your team made. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain why your team selected an option. Say one sentence aloud.

Give the reason for a past decision. Say one sentence aloud.

Mention another option you discussed. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain a decision that has a drawback. Say one sentence aloud.

Compare a quality of two things. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

We decided to wait. /
We chose this because it's simpler. /
The reason was the cost.

Speaking challenge: Explain a work decision and the reason behind it.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What would you recommend?

Colleague · question 2

Why do you prefer that option?

Colleague · question 3

Can you give me an example?

Colleague · question 4

What concern should we consider?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Report a decision your team made.
2. Explain a decision that has a drawback.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
Chưa hoàn thành
REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
