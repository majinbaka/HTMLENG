DAY 08 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Describing a problem

Mô tả vấn đề một cách rõ ràng.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Describe a recent problem: what, when, and who it affects. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

BEFORE PRACTICE · 1 MIN

Làm bài nói đầu tuần trước khi đọc bài và xem ví dụ. Ghi lại những gì bạn tự nói được.

Weekly baseline

Repeat this same 60-second task at the beginning and end of the week: Describe a work problem, its impact, and one action to try.

Làm riêng với bài nói chính nếu thời lượng khác. Tự đếm số lần dừng lâu hơn 3 giây; không suy ra độ chính xác từ thời lượng.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We're having an issue with the checkout button. It doesn't work when customers use a phone. We first noticed it this morning during testing. It affects mobile users, but the computer version is working. The expected result is for the payment page to open. Instead, the page stays the same. Right now, I'm checking the test results. I share them with our developers so they can see what happens. Could you help me with one more test? We need to understand the problem before we decide what to change.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker reporting?
2. Who is affected?
3. Why does the speaker want another test?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We're having an issue with…

Chúng tôi đang gặp vấn đề với…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Introduce a problem that needs attention.
Pattern
We're having an issue with + noun
Simple example
We're having an issue with the app.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We're having an issue with the checkout button.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It doesn't work when…

Nó không hoạt động khi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe the condition that triggers a problem.
Pattern
It doesn't work when + clause
Simple example
It doesn't work when I click here.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It doesn't work when customers use a phone.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We first noticed it…

Lần đầu chúng tôi nhận thấy lỗi là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say when a problem was first observed.
Pattern
We first noticed it + time
Simple example
We first noticed it today.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We first noticed it this morning during testing.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It affects…

Nó ảnh hưởng đến…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Identify who or what is affected.
Pattern
It affects + people / thing
Simple example
It affects mobile users.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It affects customers who use the payment page.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The expected result is…

Kết quả mong đợi là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe what should happen when the feature works.
Pattern
The expected result is + noun / for someone to + verb
Simple example
The expected result is a confirmation message.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The expected result is for the payment page to open.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. We're having an issue with…

2. It doesn't work when…

3. We first noticed it…

4. It affects…

5. The expected result is…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

We're having an issue with the app. /
It doesn't work when I click here. /
We first noticed it today.

Speaking challenge: Describe a recent problem: what, when, and who it affects.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What problem are you seeing?

Colleague · question 2

Who is affected?

Colleague · question 3

What have you checked so far?

Colleague · question 4

What do you suggest we do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Introduce a problem that needs attention.
2. Describe what should happen when the feature works.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
