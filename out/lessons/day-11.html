DAY 11 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Steps already tried

Tóm tắt các bước bạn đã thử.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Explain three troubleshooting steps and their results. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 10 · ôn sau 1 bài

Say honestly that you do not know the cause yet. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 8 · ôn sau 3 bài

Describe what should happen when the feature works. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 4 · ôn sau 7 bài

Explain who receives your work or information. Say two connected sentences aloud.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We're having an issue with the service. I've already tried restarting it, but it didn't solve the issue. First, I checked the error logs. Then, I tested the older version. The same problem happened again. I haven't tried changing the database yet because I want to make a backup first. It might be caused by the new report, but I'm not sure yet. The impact is a longer wait for our customers. Could you help me check the settings? I want to explain what I've tried so we don't repeat the same work.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker summarizing?
2. What has not been tried yet?
3. Why list the previous attempts?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I've already tried…

Tôi đã thử… rồi

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Report an action you have already tried.
Pattern
I've already tried + verb-ing
Simple example
I've already tried restarting it.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I've already tried restarting the service.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

First, I…

Đầu tiên, tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe the first action in your investigation.
Pattern
First, I + past-tense verb
Simple example
First, I checked the logs.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
First, I made a copy of the settings.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Then, I…

Sau đó, tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe the next action you took.
Pattern
Then, I + past-tense verb
Simple example
Then, I tested it again.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Then, I tested the older version.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It didn't solve the issue

Việc đó không giải quyết được lỗi

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain that a previous attempt did not work.
Pattern
It didn't solve the issue + optional next action
Simple example
It didn't solve the issue.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It didn't solve the issue, so I asked for help.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I haven't tried… yet

Tôi chưa thử…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say which action you have not tried yet.
Pattern
I haven't tried + verb-ing + yet
Simple example
I haven't tried restarting it yet.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I haven't tried changing the database yet.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I've already tried…

2. First, I…

3. Then, I…

4. It didn't solve the issue

5. I haven't tried… yet

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Report an action you have already tried. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe the first action in your investigation. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe the next action you took. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain that a previous attempt did not work. Say one sentence aloud.

Say which action you have not tried yet. Say one sentence aloud.

Name a yes-or-no question to investigate. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I've already tried restarting it. /
First, I checked the logs. /
Then, I tested it again.

Speaking challenge: Explain three troubleshooting steps and their results.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What problem are you seeing?

Colleague · question 2

Who is affected?

Colleague · question 3

What have you checked so far?

Colleague · question 4

What do you suggest we do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Report an action you have already tried.
2. Say which action you have not tried yet.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
Chưa hoàn thành
REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
