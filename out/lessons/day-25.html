DAY 25 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Plans and intentions

Trình bày kế hoạch sắp tới.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Explain your plan for next week. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 24 · ôn sau 1 bài

Name the most important lesson from an experience. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 22 · ôn sau 3 bài

Say what happened on a specific day. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 18 · ôn sau 7 bài

Describe something shared by both options. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 11 · ôn sau 14 bài

Say which action you have not tried yet. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Next week, I'm going to start a new project. I plan to meet the client on Tuesday to understand the main problem. We're meeting our designer on Wednesday morning. My goal is to make the first page simple and useful. By Friday, I want to send the first design to the client. We learned that clear instructions save time, so I will share notes after each meeting. The main reason is that everyone needs the same information. After that, we can agree what to build first. I think a small start will help us learn quickly.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker outlining?
2. When is the designer meeting?
3. Why share meeting notes?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm going to…

Tôi sẽ / tôi dự định…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
State something you intend to do.
Pattern
I'm going to + base verb
Simple example
I'm going to call the client.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm going to start a new project next week.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I plan to…

Tôi dự định…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe a planned action.
Pattern
I plan to + base verb
Simple example
I plan to test it today.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I plan to meet the client on Tuesday.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We're meeting…

Chúng tôi có lịch gặp…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe an arranged meeting.
Pattern
We're meeting + person + time
Simple example
We're meeting the client tomorrow.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We're meeting our designer on Wednesday morning.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

My goal is to…

Mục tiêu của tôi là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
State the outcome you want to achieve.
Pattern
My goal is to + base verb
Simple example
My goal is to finish today.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
My goal is to make the first page easy to use.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

By Friday, I want to…

Đến thứ Sáu, tôi muốn…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Set a target to reach by the end of the week.
Pattern
By Friday, I want to + base verb
Simple example
By Friday, I want to finish testing.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
By Friday, I want to send the first design to the client.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I'm going to…

2. I plan to…

3. We're meeting…

4. My goal is to…

5. By Friday, I want to…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

State something you intend to do. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe a planned action. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe an arranged meeting. Say one sentence aloud.

State the outcome you want to achieve. Say one sentence aloud.

Set a target to reach by the end of the week. Say one sentence aloud.

Reflect on a past decision or event. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I'm going to call the client. /
I plan to test it today. /
We're meeting the client tomorrow.

Speaking challenge: Explain your plan for next week.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What did you finish recently?

Colleague · question 2

What happened during the work?

Colleague · question 3

What did you learn?

Colleague · question 4

What are you planning next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Which chunk means “Tôi sẽ / tôi dự định…”?
2. Which chunk means “Đến thứ Sáu, tôi muốn…”?
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
