DAY 23 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Events in order

Kể sự kiện theo trình tự dễ hiểu.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Tell a short work story in a clear order. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 22 · ôn sau 1 bài

Describe the outcome of past work. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 20 · ôn sau 3 bài

Clarify something you said earlier. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 16 · ôn sau 7 bài

Give the strongest reason for your opinion. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 9 · ôn sau 14 bài

Explain a consequence of the problem. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Last week, we had a problem with the service. First, the system sent an alert while I was checking messages. Next, I opened the logs and checked the server. We found that the disk was full. After that, I asked a colleague which files we could safely remove. Then, we made a copy and removed the old temporary files. Finally, the service returned to normal. The result was a shorter wait for customers. I shared the details with our team so everyone knew what happened. We decided to check the disk regularly to avoid the same problem.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker describing?
2. What was wrong with the server?
3. Why check the disk regularly now?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

First, I…

Đầu tiên…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Begin a story about a sequence of events.
Pattern
First, + past-tense clause
Simple example
First, I saw an alert.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
First, I checked the message from the system.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Next, I…

Tiếp theo…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Introduce the next action in a story.
Pattern
Next, + past-tense clause
Simple example
Next, I checked the server.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Next, I asked a colleague to check the settings.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

After that, I…

Sau đó…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Move on to a later event.
Pattern
After that, + past-tense clause
Simple example
After that, I called the team.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
After that, we found some old files on the server.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Then, I…

Rồi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Link another action in the same sequence.
Pattern
Then, + past-tense clause
Simple example
Then, I removed the old files.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Then, we tested the service together.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Finally, we…

Cuối cùng…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe the final event or outcome.
Pattern
Finally, + past-tense clause
Simple example
Finally, we fixed the page.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Finally, we got the service working again.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. First, I…

2. Next, I…

3. After that, I…

4. Then, I…

5. Finally, we…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

First, I saw an alert. /
Next, I checked the server. /
After that, I called the team.

Speaking challenge: Tell a short work story in a clear order.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What did you finish recently?

Colleague · question 2

What happened during the work?

Colleague · question 3

What did you learn?

Colleague · question 4

What are you planning next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
