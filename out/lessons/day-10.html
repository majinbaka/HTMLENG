DAY 10 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Possible causes

Nói về nguyên nhân khi chưa chắc chắn.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Suggest two possible causes without sounding too certain. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 9 · ôn sau 1 bài

Describe how long an action takes. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 7 · ôn sau 3 bài

Tell a colleague about your current project. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 3 · ôn sau 7 bài

Name your main priority for this week. Say two connected sentences aloud.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We're having an issue with the database. It might be caused by the new report, but I'm not sure yet. One possibility is that too many users are opening it at the same time. We need to check whether the problem started after the report changed. It probably isn't the network because other pages open quickly. The main symptom is a long wait for data. As a result, support agents cannot finish their work quickly. Right now, I'm checking the older version. Let's look at it together before we decide what caused it.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the team discussing?
2. Why is the network less likely to be the cause?
3. Why does the speaker say they are not sure yet?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It might be caused by…

Nguyên nhân có thể là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Suggest a possible cause without claiming certainty.
Pattern
It might be caused by + noun
Simple example
It might be caused by the update.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It might be caused by the new report.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

One possibility is…

Một khả năng là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Offer one explanation to investigate.
Pattern
One possibility is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
One possibility is a full disk.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
One possibility is that too many people are using the app.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm not sure yet

Tôi chưa chắc

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say honestly that you do not know the cause yet.
Pattern
I'm not sure yet + optional next action
Simple example
I'm not sure yet.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm not sure yet. I need to check the logs.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We need to check whether…

Chúng ta cần kiểm tra xem…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name a yes-or-no question to investigate.
Pattern
We need to check whether + clause
Simple example
We need to check whether it works.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We need to check whether the new report is causing the delay.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It probably isn't…

Có lẽ không phải là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tentatively rule out a possible cause.
Pattern
It probably isn't + noun
Simple example
It probably isn't the network.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It probably isn't the network because other pages work.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. It might be caused by…

2. One possibility is…

3. I'm not sure yet

4. We need to check whether…

5. It probably isn't…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Suggest a possible cause without claiming certainty. Say one sentence aloud.

Offer one explanation to investigate. Say one sentence aloud.

Say honestly that you do not know the cause yet. Say one sentence aloud.

Name a yes-or-no question to investigate. Say one sentence aloud.

Tentatively rule out a possible cause. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain a consequence of the problem. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

It might be caused by the update. /
One possibility is a full disk. /
I'm not sure yet.

Speaking challenge: Suggest two possible causes without sounding too certain.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What problem are you seeing?

Colleague · question 2

Who is affected?

Colleague · question 3

What have you checked so far?

Colleague · question 4

What do you suggest we do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Suggest a possible cause without claiming certainty.
2. Tentatively rule out a possible cause.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

Today's progress
