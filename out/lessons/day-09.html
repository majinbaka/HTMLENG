DAY 09 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Symptoms and impact

Giải thích triệu chứng và tác động.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Explain the symptoms and business impact of a problem. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 8 · ôn sau 1 bài

Introduce a problem that needs attention. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 6 · ôn sau 3 bài

Describe something you cannot do easily. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 2 · ôn sau 7 bài

Name your first action at work. Say two connected sentences aloud.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We're having an issue with the dashboard. The main symptom is that pages open very slowly. It takes ten seconds to load a customer page. As a result, our support agents cannot answer customers quickly. This means that customers have to wait longer, especially during busy hours. The impact is lost time for both customers and the support team. We first noticed it yesterday afternoon. Right now, I'm checking whether every page is slow. I share the results with the developers. Could you help me with a test after lunch?
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the main problem?
2. How long does a customer page take to load?
3. Why does the speaker mention waiting customers?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The main symptom is…

Dấu hiệu chính là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name the most noticeable sign of the problem.
Pattern
The main symptom is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
The main symptom is a slow page.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The main symptom is that the dashboard takes too long to open.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It takes… to…

Mất… để…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe how long an action takes.
Pattern
It takes + time + to + base verb
Simple example
It takes ten seconds to load.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It takes ten seconds to open a customer page.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

As a result…

Kết quả là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain a consequence of the problem.
Pattern
As a result, + clause
Simple example
As a result, we are behind schedule.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
As a result, customers have to wait longer.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

This means that…

Điều này có nghĩa là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain the practical meaning of a situation.
Pattern
This means that + clause
Simple example
This means that we need more time.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
This means that support agents cannot answer quickly.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The impact is…

Tác động là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Summarize the effect on people or work.
Pattern
The impact is + noun phrase
Simple example
The impact is a longer wait.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The impact is a longer wait for our customers.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. The main symptom is…

2. It takes… to…

3. As a result…

4. This means that…

5. The impact is…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Name the most noticeable sign of the problem. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe how long an action takes. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain a consequence of the problem. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain the practical meaning of a situation. Say one sentence aloud.

Summarize the effect on people or work. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe the condition that triggers a problem. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

The main symptom is a slow page. /
It takes ten seconds to load. /
As a result, we are behind schedule.

Speaking challenge: Explain the symptoms and business impact of a problem.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What problem are you seeing?

Colleague · question 2

Who is affected?

Colleague · question 3

What have you checked so far?

Colleague · question 4

What do you suggest we do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Name the most noticeable sign of the problem.
2. Summarize the effect on people or work.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
Chưa hoàn thành
REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
