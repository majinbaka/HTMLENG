DAY 28 · WEEKLY REVIEW · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Ôn tập tuần 4
Final work update

Kết hợp quá khứ, hiện tại và tương lai.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Give a two-minute final update about your work and learning. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

WEEK 4 · REVIEW & PRACTICE

Ôn lại toàn bộ tuần 4

Nhớ lại 30 chunk và mẫu câu từ Day 22–27, rồi vận dụng trong bài đọc, bài nói và hội thoại tổng kết bên dưới. Năm chunk trọng tâm hôm nay đều là chunk đã học.

Routine chính: 30 phút. Ngân hàng thêm 90 bài luyện: 30 nhớ lại & nói, 30 điền từ, 30 biến đổi câu. Mỗi lần chọn khoảng 15 câu trong 10–15 phút; có thể quay lại nhiều lần, không cần làm cả 90 câu trong một buổi.

Câu trả lời và các lần thử được tự động lưu, đi cùng file xuất tiến độ. Bài luyện thêm không làm tăng streak; hoàn thành bài bằng routine chính cùng đọc hiểu, nói và quiz.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Here's my final update. Last week, I completed the customer report. We found that people needed a simpler way to pay. I'm currently working on the payment page with our design team. We learned that testing early helps us find small problems before they become serious. First, we checked the main screen. Then, we asked two customers to try it. Their feedback helped us make the instructions clearer. The next step is to test the mobile version. I think we are on track, and I plan to share the results on Friday. What would you like me to explain in more detail?
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What does this update cover?
2. What is the next step?
3. Why invite another question?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Ôn Day 22

Last week, I…

Tuần trước tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Start a short account of last week's work.
Pattern
Last week, I + past-tense verb
Simple example
Last week, I tested the app.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Last week, I talked to five customers.
2/5 · Ôn Day 3

Pattern

Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm currently working on our payment page.

VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. Last week, I…

2. I'm currently working on…

3. We learned that…

4. The next step is…

5. I plan to…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Start a short account of last week's work. Say one sentence aloud.

Tell a colleague about your current project. Say one sentence aloud.

Share a lesson from a past experience. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain what should happen next. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe a planned action. Say one sentence aloud.

Report completed work that matters now. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

Last week, I tested the app. /
I'm currently working on a new page. /
We learned that testing takes time.

Speaking challenge: Give a two-minute final update about your work and learning.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What did you finish recently?

Colleague · question 2

What happened during the work?

Colleague · question 3

What did you learn?

Colleague · question 4

What are you planning next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
