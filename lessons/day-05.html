MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Give a stand-up update: yesterday, today, and blockers. Speak for 90–120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Here's my update. Yesterday, I finished the login screen. Today, I'm going to connect it to the server. I'm still working on the mobile version, so it isn't ready to test yet. One blocker is the missing API documentation. I need that documentation from the other team. I usually start by checking our task board, and I share any questions with the product manager. The deadline is Friday. After this meeting, I'm going to ask when the documentation will be ready. At the end of the day, I'll send another short update.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the purpose of the update?
2. What does the speaker need?
3. Why will the speaker contact the other team?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Yesterday, I finished…

Hôm qua tôi đã hoàn thành…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Report a task you finished yesterday.
Pattern
Yesterday, I finished + noun / verb-ing
Simple example
Yesterday, I finished the report.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Yesterday, I finished testing the login page.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Today, I'm going to…

Hôm nay tôi sẽ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
State your plan for today.
Pattern
Today, I'm going to + base verb
Simple example
Today, I'm going to test the app.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Today, I'm going to connect the page to the server.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm still working on…

Tôi vẫn đang làm…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain which task is not finished yet.
Pattern
I'm still working on + noun
Simple example
I'm still working on the report.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm still working on the mobile version.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

One blocker is…

Một trở ngại là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name something that is stopping your progress.
Pattern
One blocker is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
One blocker is the missing file.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
One blocker is that I don't have the test account.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I need… from…

Tôi cần… từ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tell a colleague what you need and from whom.
Pattern
I need + thing + from + person / team
Simple example
I need help from my team.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I need the API documentation from the other team.

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

2. Today, I'm going to…

3. I'm still working on…

4. One blocker is…

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

Yesterday, I finished the report. /
Today, I'm going to test the app. /
I'm still working on the report.

Speaking challenge: Give a stand-up update: yesterday, today, and blockers.

Speak for 90–120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What do you do in your team?

Colleague · question 2

What are you working on now?

Colleague · question 3

What is difficult about it?

Colleague · question 4

What will you do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
