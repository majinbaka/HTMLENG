MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Describe a normal workday from morning to evening. Speak for 30–60 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Hi, I'm Leo. I work as a developer, and I'm part of the product team. I usually start by checking messages at eight thirty. The first thing I do is read anything urgent from my team. After that, I plan my tasks. I often write code before lunch because the office is quiet. In the afternoon, I help our team test new pages. At the end of the day, I send a short update. My main responsibility is building useful tools, but I also make time to help a colleague when they get stuck.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is Leo describing?
2. When does Leo often write code?
3. Why does Leo like coding in the morning?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I usually start by…

Tôi thường bắt đầu bằng việc…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe how you begin your workday.
Pattern
I usually start by + verb-ing
Simple example
I usually start by checking messages.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I usually start by reviewing my task list.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The first thing I do is…

Việc đầu tiên tôi làm là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name your first action at work.
Pattern
The first thing I do is + base verb
Simple example
The first thing I do is check my email.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The first thing I do is read messages from my team.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

After that, I…

Sau đó, tôi…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Move to the next step in your routine.
Pattern

Simple example

Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn

VARIATION · 4 MIN

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

2. The first thing I do is…

3. After that, I…

4. I often…

5. At the end of the day…

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I usually start by checking messages. /
The first thing I do is check my email. /
After that, I check my tasks.

Speaking challenge: Describe a normal workday from morning to evening.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What do you do in your team?

Colleague · question 2

What are you working on now?

Colleague · question 3

What is difficult about it?

Colleague · question 4

What will you do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
