DAY 18 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Comparing options

So sánh lựa chọn bằng tiêu chí rõ ràng.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Compare two tools or approaches you know. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Explain what information your team tracks. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We need a new chat tool. Compared with Tool A, Tool B costs more. However, Tool B is more secure than Tool A. Both options connect to our calendar, so either one could work for meetings. The main difference is how they protect customer information. In my opinion, Tool B is the better choice because our team handles private customer details. I agree that the price matters, but security matters more here. Could we test Tool B with one small team first? That would help us check whether it is easy to use before we make a final decision.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the team comparing?
2. What do both options connect to?
3. Why does the speaker prefer Tool B?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Compared with…

So với…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Compare one option with another.
Pattern
Compared with + noun, + clause
Simple example
Compared with A, B is cheaper.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Compared with our old tool, this one is easier to use.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

… is more… than…

… hơn…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Compare a quality of two things.
Pattern
A + is more + long adjective + than + B
Simple example
This tool is more useful than that one.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Tool B is more secure than Tool A.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Both options…

Cả hai lựa chọn đều…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe something shared by both options.
Pattern
Both options + plural verb
Simple example
Both options work.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Both options connect to our calendar.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The main difference is…

Khác biệt chính là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Identify the most important difference.
Pattern
The main difference is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
The main difference is the price.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The main difference is that Tool B has better security.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

… is the better choice because…

… là lựa chọn tốt hơn vì…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Choose an option and explain your reason.
Pattern
Option + is the better choice because + clause
Simple example
A is the better choice because it's cheaper.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Tool B is the better choice because it protects our data.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. Compared with…

2. … is more… than…

3. Both options…

4. The main difference is…

5. … is the better choice because…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Compare one option with another. Say one sentence aloud.

Compare a quality of two things. Say one sentence aloud.

Describe something shared by both options. Say one sentence aloud.

Identify the most important difference. Say one sentence aloud.

Choose an option and explain your reason. Say one sentence aloud.

Say which part of an opinion you agree with. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

Compared with A, B is cheaper. /
This tool is more useful than that one. /
Both options work.

Speaking challenge: Compare two tools or approaches you know.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What would you recommend?

Colleague · question 2

Why do you prefer that option?

Colleague · question 3

Can you give me an example?

Colleague · question 4

What concern should we consider?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Which chunk means “So với…”?
2. Which chunk means “… là lựa chọn tốt hơn vì…”?
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
