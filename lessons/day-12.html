DAY 12 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Asking for help

Yêu cầu trợ giúp hoặc làm rõ lịch sự.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Ask a colleague for the information you need. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 11 · ôn sau 1 bài

Explain that a previous attempt did not work. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 9 · ôn sau 3 bài

Name the most noticeable sign of the problem. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 5 · ôn sau 7 bài

Tell a colleague what you need and from whom. Say two connected sentences aloud.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Yuki: We're having an issue with the report, but I can't make the problem happen on my computer. Could you clarify the last step?
Lee: I click Save, and the page goes blank.
Yuki: Can you show me the error? Which browser did you use?
Lee: Chrome, on my laptop.
Yuki: Does it happen when you use another browser?
Lee: I haven't tried that yet.
Yuki: Could you help me check that now? We need to check whether it affects one browser or every browser. After that, I'll share the results with our developers so they have clear details.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is Yuki trying to do?
2. Which browser did Lee use?
3. Why test another browser?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Could you clarify…?

Bạn làm rõ… được không?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Ask for a more precise explanation.
Pattern
Could you clarify + noun / question-word clause + ?
Simple example
Could you clarify the last step?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Could you clarify what happens after you click Save?
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Can you show me…?

Bạn chỉ cho tôi… được không?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Ask someone to demonstrate or show something.
Pattern
Can you show me + noun / how to + verb + ?
Simple example
Can you show me the error?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Can you show me how to open the report?
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Which… did you use?

Bạn đã dùng… nào?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Ask which specific tool or option was used.
Pattern
Which + noun + did you use?
Simple example
Which browser did you use?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Which account did you use to test the page?
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Does it happen when…?

Lỗi có xảy ra khi… không?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Check the condition in which a problem occurs.
Pattern
Does it happen when + clause + ?
Simple example
Does it happen when you log in?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Does it happen when you use another browser?
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Could you help me check…?

Bạn giúp tôi kiểm tra… được không?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Invite someone to check something with you.
Pattern
Could you help me check + noun / whether-clause + ?
Simple example
Could you help me check this page?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Could you help me check whether the issue affects mobile users?
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. Could you clarify…?

2. Can you show me…?

3. Which… did you use?

4. Does it happen when…?

5. Could you help me check…?

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Ask for a more precise explanation. Say one sentence aloud.

Ask someone to demonstrate or show something. Say one sentence aloud.

Ask which specific tool or option was used. Say one sentence aloud.

Check the condition in which a problem occurs. Say one sentence aloud.

Invite someone to check something with you. Say one sentence aloud.

Say which action you have not tried yet. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

Could you clarify the last step? /
Can you show me the error? /
Which browser did you use?

Speaking challenge: Ask a colleague for the information you need.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What problem are you seeing?

Colleague · question 2

Who is affected?

Colleague · question 3

What have you checked so far?

Colleague · question 4

What do you suggest we do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Ask for a more precise explanation.
2. Invite someone to check something with you.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
