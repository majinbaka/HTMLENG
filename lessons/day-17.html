DAY 17 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Agreeing politely

Đồng ý hoặc không đồng ý mà vẫn hợp tác.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Respond politely to an idea you partly disagree with. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 16 · ôn sau 1 bài

Name a specific example that supports your view. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 14 · ôn sau 3 bài

Say when a problem was first observed. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 10 · ôn sau 7 bài

Tentatively rule out a possible cause. Say two connected sentences aloud.

Day 3 · ôn sau 14 bài

Say what you are doing at this moment. Answer as if a colleague has just asked you.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Kai: I agree that the deadline is important. I think we can release the main feature on Friday.
Jo: Customers are expecting all the smaller features too.
Kai: That's a good point. I see what you mean, but we still need time to test them.
Jo: Maybe we can skip the final tests.
Kai: I'm not sure I agree. The main reason is that we don't want customers to find serious problems. Could we add the smaller features next week instead?
Jo: Yes, that sounds possible. Let's explain the plan to the product manager and check what customers need most.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What are Kai and Jo trying to agree on?
2. What does Kai suggest for the smaller features?
3. Why does Kai disagree about skipping tests?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I agree that…

Tôi đồng ý rằng…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say which part of an opinion you agree with.
Pattern
I agree that + clause
Simple example
I agree that we need more time.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I agree that the deadline is important.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

That's a good point

Ý đó hợp lý

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Acknowledge a useful point from a colleague.
Pattern
That's a good point + optional response
Simple example
That's a good point.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
That's a good point. We should test it first.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I see what you mean, but…

Tôi hiểu ý bạn, nhưng…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Recognize an opinion before expressing a concern.
Pattern
I see what you mean, but + clause
Simple example
I see what you mean, but it's expensive.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I see what you mean, but we need more time to test.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm not sure I agree

Tôi chưa chắc mình đồng ý

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Disagree gently without attacking the speaker.
Pattern
I'm not sure I agree + optional reason
Simple example
I'm not sure I agree.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm not sure I agree. Customers still need that feature.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Could we… instead?

Thay vào đó, chúng ta có thể… không?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Suggest an alternative politely.
Pattern
Could we + base verb + instead?
Simple example
Could we meet tomorrow instead?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Could we release the main feature first instead?
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I agree that…

2. That's a good point

3. I see what you mean, but…

4. I'm not sure I agree

5. Could we… instead?

Make the language yours

Speaking challenge: Respond politely to an idea you partly disagree with.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What would you recommend?

Colleague · question 2

Why do you prefer that option?

Colleague · question 3

Can you give me an example?

Colleague · question 4

What concern should we consider?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
