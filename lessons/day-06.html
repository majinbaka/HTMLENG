DAY 06 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Colleague conversation

Phản xạ trong hội thoại với đồng nghiệp.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Role-play both sides of a short conversation about a task. Speak for 90–120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

Day 5 · ôn sau 1 bài

Explain which task is not finished yet. Say one sentence from memory.

Day 3 · ôn sau 3 bài

Tell someone when a task must be ready. Use it in a different project or situation.

Day 5 · ôn sau 1 bài

Report a task you finished yesterday. Say one sentence from memory.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Ana: How's it going? Is the new page ready?
Minh: It's almost ready. Yesterday, I finished the main screen. I'm still working on one test.
Ana: What's the problem?
Minh: I'm having trouble with the login feature. It works on my computer, but the mobile test fails. Could you help me with that test?
Ana: Sure. Let's look at it together after lunch.
Minh: Thanks. Right now, I'm checking the error message. I share test results with our team, so everyone can see the problem. The deadline is Friday, and this is the last thing we need to check.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is Minh doing?
2. What is failing?
3. Why does Ana suggest looking together?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

How's it going?

Dạo này công việc thế nào?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Start a friendly conversation about progress.
Pattern
How's it going? + optional follow-up question
Simple example
How's it going?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
How's it going? Is the new page ready?
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It's almost ready

Gần xong rồi

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tell a colleague that only a little work remains.
Pattern
It's almost ready + optional remaining task
Simple example
It's almost ready.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It's almost ready. I just need to run one test.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm having trouble with…

Tôi đang gặp khó khăn với…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe something you cannot do easily.
Pattern
I'm having trouble with + noun / verb-ing
Simple example
I'm having trouble with this test.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm having trouble with the login feature.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Could you help me with…?

Bạn giúp tôi… được không?

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Ask politely for help with a task.
Pattern
Could you help me with + noun / verb-ing + ?
Simple example
Could you help me with this task?
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Could you help me with testing the new page?
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

Let's look at it together

Chúng ta cùng xem nhé

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Suggest checking a problem together.
Pattern
Let's look at it together + optional time
Simple example
Let's look at it together.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
Let's look at it together after lunch.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What do you do in your team?

Colleague · question 2

What are you working on now?

Colleague · question 3

What is difficult about it?

Colleague · question 4

What will you do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
