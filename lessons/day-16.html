DAY 16 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Reasons and examples

Hỗ trợ ý kiến bằng lý do và ví dụ.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

State an opinion, then add a reason and example. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Recall before opening today’s chunk cards. Ôn theo mốc bài học +1, +3, +7, +14; mỗi lần chọn vài chunk để giữ bài gọn.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

I think shorter meetings help our team. The main reason is that everyone needs time to focus. Long meetings often feel less useful. This is because people repeat the same points. For example, our daily meeting now takes fifteen minutes, and we still share the important updates. A good example is yesterday's meeting: we named one blocker and agreed who would help. That's why I prefer a short meeting with a clear purpose. In my opinion, difficult questions can go into a separate conversation. We can try this for a week and compare how much useful work we finish.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What does the speaker support?
2. How long is the daily meeting now?
3. Why mention yesterday's meeting?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The main reason is…

Lý do chính là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Give the strongest reason for your opinion.
Pattern
The main reason is + noun / that + clause
Simple example
The main reason is the cost.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The main reason is that shorter meetings save time.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

This is because…

Đó là vì…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain why your previous statement is true.
Pattern
This is because + clause
Simple example
This is because the app is simple.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
This is because everyone can see the same task list.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

For example…

Ví dụ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Support your point with a concrete example.
Pattern
For example, + clause
Simple example
For example, we can meet online.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
For example, our daily meeting now takes fifteen minutes.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

A good example is…

Một ví dụ điển hình là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name a specific example that supports your view.
Pattern
A good example is + noun
Simple example
A good example is our team.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
A good example is our short Monday meeting.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

That's why…

Đó là lý do…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Connect a reason to its result.
Pattern
That's why + clause
Simple example
That's why I agree.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
That's why I prefer a short daily meeting.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. The main reason is…

2. This is because…

3. For example…

4. A good example is…

5. That's why…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

Colleague · question 2

Colleague · question 3

Colleague · question 4

What concern should we consider?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
