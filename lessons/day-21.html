Today's mission

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

I'd recommend testing the new process with one small team. I think changing everything at once would be difficult. The main reason is that we don't know how much help people will need. For example, a small test lets us answer questions before the whole company starts. I agree that we want to move quickly. However, we also need time to learn. We decided to ask one team to try it for a week. After that, we can compare the results and choose the next step. What do you think would be most useful to measure during that week?
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What does the speaker recommend?
2. How long will the trial last?
3. Why ask what to measure?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 4

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
