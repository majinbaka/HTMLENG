INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

We've completed the main screens for the mobile app. So far, we've tested the login and payment pages. We're on track to release on Friday, but we still need to fix three small bugs. The next step is to test those changes and prepare the store description. Last week, we found that some instructions were unclear. We learned that short examples help, so we added them. I think the app will be easier to use now. Could you help me with one final check tomorrow? After that, I plan to send a short update to the product manager.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker reporting?
2. What still needs fixing?
3. Why ask for one final check?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We've completed…

Chúng tôi đã hoàn thành…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Report completed work that matters now.
Pattern
We've completed + noun
Simple example
We've completed the main screens.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We've completed the first round of testing.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

So far, we've…

Tính đến hiện tại, chúng tôi đã…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Summarize progress up to now.
Pattern
So far, we've + past participle
Simple example
So far, we've fixed two bugs.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
So far, we've tested all the main screens.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We're on track

Chúng tôi đang đúng tiến độ

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Say that work is progressing according to plan.
Pattern
We're on track + optional to + verb
Simple example
We're on track.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We're on track to release the app on Friday.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We still need to…

Chúng tôi vẫn cần…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name work that remains unfinished.
Pattern
We still need to + base verb
Simple example
We still need to test it.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We still need to fix three small bugs.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

The next step is…

Bước tiếp theo là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain what should happen next.
Pattern
The next step is to + base verb
Simple example
The next step is to test it.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
The next step is to share the app with a small group.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

Colleague · question 2

Colleague · question 3

Colleague · question 4

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.
