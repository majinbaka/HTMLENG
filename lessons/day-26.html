Predictions

Đưa ra dự đoán có mức độ chắc chắn phù hợp.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Make three predictions about a project or your work. Speak for 120 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

I think more customers will use the app after the launch. We expect more support questions in the first week. The new help page is ready, and it will probably answer some simple questions. It may take a few days for customers to find everything. I'm confident that our support team can help them. We learned that clear instructions are useful, so we added short examples to the page. My goal is to make the first week easier. On Friday, we will check the number of questions and decide whether the help page needs any changes.
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. What is the speaker discussing?
2. What will probably answer simple questions?
3. Why check support questions on Friday?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.

CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I think… will…

Tôi nghĩ… sẽ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Make a simple prediction.
Pattern
I think + subject + will + base verb
Simple example
I think it will work.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I think more customers will use the app next month.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

We expect…

Chúng tôi kỳ vọng…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe an expected result.
Pattern
We expect + noun / to + verb / that-clause
Simple example
We expect more users.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
We expect the first week to be busy.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It will probably…

Có lẽ nó sẽ…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Make a prediction that is likely but not certain.
Pattern
It will probably + base verb
Simple example
It will probably take an hour.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It will probably reduce simple support questions.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

It may…

Nó có thể…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Describe a possible future result.
Pattern
It may + base verb
Simple example
It may take longer.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
It may take a few days for users to learn the new page.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm confident that…

Tôi tin chắc rằng…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Express confidence about an outcome.
Pattern
I'm confident that + clause
Simple example
I'm confident that we can finish.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm confident that the new help page will be useful.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I think… will…

2. We expect…

3. It will probably…

4. It may…

5. I'm confident that…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Describe an expected result. Say one sentence aloud.

Express confidence about an outcome. Say one sentence aloud.

State the outcome you want to achieve. Say one sentence from memory.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I think it will work. /
We expect more users. /
It will probably take an hour.

Speaking challenge: Make three predictions about a project or your work.

Speak for 120 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What did you finish recently?

Colleague · question 2

What happened during the work?

Colleague · question 3

What did you learn?

Colleague · question 4

What are you planning next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.
