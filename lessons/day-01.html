00 · WARM-UP · 3 MIN

Warm up your English

01 · SHORT READING · 5 MIN

Read for the big picture

Read once without translating every word. What is the situation?

Mai is a software developer at a logistics company. She works with four engineers and a product manager. Her main responsibility is building tools that help drivers plan better routes.
