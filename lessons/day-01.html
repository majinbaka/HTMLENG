DAY 01 · 30 MINUTES · 6 SESSIONS / WEEK

Talking about my job

Nói về vai trò, đội nhóm và trách nhiệm chính.

MISSION · 1 MIN

Today's mission

Introduce yourself: your role, team, and two responsibilities. Speak for 30–60 seconds, using at least 3 of today’s 5 chunks. Say it first; write only brief notes.

5 chunk mỗi ngày · tối đa 5 chunk mới. Ngày tổng kết ôn lại chunk đã học.

BEFORE PRACTICE · 1 MIN

Làm bài nói đầu tuần trước khi đọc bài và xem ví dụ. Ghi lại những gì bạn tự nói được.

Weekly baseline

Repeat this same 60-second task at the beginning and end of the week: Introduce your role, team, and main responsibility.

Làm riêng với bài nói chính nếu thời lượng khác. Tự đếm số lần dừng lâu hơn 3 giây; không suy ra độ chính xác từ thời lượng.

RECALL · 3 MIN

Warm-up recall

Say your job, your team, and one task aloud. Use any English you already know. This is a starting point, not a test.

INPUT · 4 MIN

Read, listen to your own voice, and notice

Read once for meaning. Then read aloud; on the second pass, notice how the speaker uses whole phrases.

Hi, I'm Mai. I work as a software developer at a logistics company. I'm part of the product team. My main responsibility is building tools that help drivers plan their routes. I mainly work with four engineers and a product manager. I help our team solve customer problems. Most mornings, we talk about what needs to be done. Today, we need to make a page easier to use. I like seeing how a small change can make someone's work easier. What about you? What do you do?
COMPREHENSION · 3 MIN

Check the message

1. Why is Mai speaking?
2. Who does Mai mainly work with?
3. Why does Mai ask what you do?
Look for who, what happened, and why the speaker says it.
Chưa hoàn thành
CHUNKS · 5 MIN

Five chunks to use today

Learn the whole phrase. Change the details to fit your work. Mở từng thẻ khi cần; sau đó nhìn đi chỗ khác và nói lại.

1/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I work as a…

Tôi làm nghề…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Introduce your job to a new colleague.
Pattern
I work as a/an + job
Simple example
I work as a developer.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I work as a developer on a small product team.
2/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I'm part of the… team

Tôi thuộc nhóm…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Tell someone which team you belong to.
Pattern
I'm part of the + team name + team
Simple example
I'm part of the support team.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I'm part of the mobile team at my company.
3/5 · Luyện hôm nay

My main responsibility is…

Trách nhiệm chính của tôi là…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain the main thing you are responsible for.
Pattern
My main responsibility is + verb-ing
Simple example
My main responsibility is testing the app.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
My main responsibility is helping customers use our app.
4/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I mainly work with…

Tôi chủ yếu làm việc với…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Name the people you work with most.
Pattern
I mainly work with + people
Simple example
I mainly work with designers.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I mainly work with two developers and a tester.
5/5 · Luyện hôm nay

I help our team…

Tôi giúp nhóm…

Cách dùng & ví dụ
When to use
Explain how you contribute to the team.
Pattern
I help our team + base verb
Simple example
I help our team solve problems.
Work example · đổi chi tiết cho đúng với bạn
I help our team release useful features.
VARIATION · 4 MIN

Change it, then make it yours

Say each answer first, then type a short sentence. Two quick variations per chunk; keep the same pattern.

1. I work as a…

2. I'm part of the… team

3. My main responsibility is…

4. I mainly work with…

5. I help our team…

RAPID RECALL · 3 MIN

Recall in about three seconds

Look away from the chunk cards. Try to start speaking within 3 seconds, then type what you said. A model appears only after an attempt; other natural answers are possible.

Introduce your job to a new colleague. Say one sentence aloud.

Tell someone which team you belong to. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain the main thing you are responsible for. Say one sentence aloud.

Name the people you work with most. Say one sentence aloud.

Explain how you contribute to the team. Say one sentence aloud.

SPEAK · 5 MIN

Make the language yours

Read aloud three times. Follow the pauses marked by / and emphasize the important words.

I work as a developer. /
I'm part of the support team. /
My main responsibility is testing the app.

Speaking challenge: Introduce yourself: your role, team, and two responsibilities.

Speak for 30–60 seconds. Use at least 3 chunks. Notes only: your situation · two details · a reason or problem · your next step. Do not write a full script first.

Chưa hoàn thành
CONVERSATION · 3 MIN

A real work conversation

Practise with a partner, or take both roles aloud. Your partner is a coworker. Open one question, answer it, then continue. Khi luyện cùng Codex, yêu cầu đóng vai đồng nghiệp và hỏi từng câu; trang này không có AI trò chuyện trực tiếp.

Colleague · question 1

What do you do in your team?

Colleague · question 2

What are you working on now?

Colleague · question 3

What is difficult about it?

Colleague · question 4

What will you do next?

After 4 exchanges, choose at most 3 useful corrections. Say the improved sentences once more.

END CHECK · 2 MIN

Choose a useful chunk

1. Introduce your job to a new colleague.
2. Explain how you contribute to the team.
Think about the situation where you would use the phrase.

REVIEW · 2 MIN

Today's progress

Các số về nói và nhớ chunk là tự đánh giá; số câu đọc hiểu lấy từ lần nộp gần nhất. Chưa nhập nghĩa là chưa có dữ liệu.
